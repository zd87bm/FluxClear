FluxClear Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FluxClear handles information when you use the Android application.

1. Core functionality

FluxClear is a utility app designed to help you understand and manage storage space on your Android device. It can review storage usage, scan for cache, temporary files, duplicate files, similar photos, large files, and help you review recoverable photos, videos, audio files, and documents where Android makes recovery possible.

2. Information involved in app features

The information involved depends on the feature you choose. FluxClear may access or process the following information to display results, complete selected actions, and keep the app experience understandable:

  • Device storage details, such as used space, available space, file sizes, file types, and scan times.
  • File details, such as file names, paths, extensions, sizes, modified times, and category labels shown in scan results.
  • Media details for photos, videos, audio, and documents, including thumbnails or media records needed to show reviewable or recoverable items.
  • Your in-app selections, such as the files or categories you choose to clean, delete, or recover.
  • Basic app preferences, such as display language and startup guidance state.

3. How this information is used

FluxClear uses the information described above for the following purposes:

  • To calculate and display storage usage and scan results.
  • To group files into practical review categories such as cache, temporary files, duplicates, similar photos, large files, and recoverable items.
  • To perform actions you confirm, including cleaning, deleting, or recovering selected items.
  • To remember basic preferences that make the app easier to use.

4. Permissions and why they are requested

FluxClear requests permissions only when they are relevant to a feature you open or a page that needs them.

  • All Files Access / storage permission: used to scan files, show cleanable or recoverable items, remove selected items after confirmation, and restore supported files where Android allows recovery.
  • Read external storage: used on supported Android versions to read files and media needed for scan, cleanup, and recovery features.
  • Write external storage: used on supported Android versions when a selected cleanup or recovery action requires writing or updating files.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES: used on Android 13 (API level 33) and above to read image files from shared media storage, supporting features including photo scanning, similar photo detection, cleanup review, and recoverable image identification.
  • READ_MEDIA_VIDEO: used on Android 13 (API level 33) and above to read video files from shared media storage, supporting features including video scanning, large video file screening, cleanup review, and recoverable video identification.
  • Internet: used to open this Privacy Policy page and related policy configuration.

5. Cleanup, deletion, and recovery actions

FluxClear shows scan results before you take action. Cleanup or deletion applies only to selected items and requires confirmation before files are removed.

Some files may not be recoverable because recovery depends on Android storage rules, MediaStore records, file availability, and device manufacturer behavior.

6. Third-party services and linked destinations

When you choose related actions, FluxClear may open Google Play, a web browser, Android system settings, or other system-provided pages. Those destinations are operated by their respective providers and may handle information according to their own privacy policies and settings.

7. Your choices and controls

  • You can grant or deny storage-related permissions through Android permission dialogs or system settings.
  • You can review scan results and change selected items before cleanup or deletion.
  • You can remove the app from your device using Android system controls.

8. Children

FluxClear is a general utility app and is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, legal requirements, or operational needs change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when this Privacy Policy was last updated.
